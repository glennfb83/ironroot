Profile

Method & sources. Rank inputs use estimated 1RM (Epley), lift-specific reference curves inspired by judged powerlifting results in OpenPowerlifting and the Strength Initiative. Age bands follow competition standards; body mass uses an allometric relative-strength adjustment. Height is a small, bounded anthropometric adjustment. These are comparison estimates, not medical advice or a prediction of ability. See Suchomel et al. (2018) for the relationship between strength and performance and LeSuer et al. (2008) for 1RM estimation validation.
